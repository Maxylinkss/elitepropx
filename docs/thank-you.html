Enquiry sent

The message has left your browser and is on its way. You will get a reply at the address you gave — usually within one business day, and often the same day during European and US market hours.

What the reply will contain

  • The flat $220 fee, confirmed for the firm and account size you named.
  • A timeline in trading days rather than calendar days, because that is what the rulebook governs.
  • A compliance note on your firm's current position on third-party trading, including the honest answer when that position makes the arrangement a bad idea.
  • Any question whose answer depends on your firm's terms — asked before a fee is quoted, not after it is paid.

If your firm's rules have changed recently, say so in the follow-up. Timelines and quotes are checked against current published terms, and a rule change can move both.

If you forgot something

Reply to the email when it arrives, or send a second message to support@elitepropx.com. The details that change the answer most are the exact plan you are buying, how previous attempts ended, and anything you know about instrument, news or weekend restrictions at your firm.

Nothing is charged at this stage, and an enquiry carries no obligation.

Questions after sending

When will I hear back?

Within one business day in most cases, and often the same day during European and US market hours. A question that needs your firm's current terms checked can take a little longer, because the answer is verified against documentation rather than recalled.

I sent the wrong account size or firm — what now?

Send a second message or reply to the confirmation email. Nothing is charged at this stage, so a correction costs nothing and the quote is rebuilt against the right rulebook.

Do I need to send anything else now?

No. Evaluation credentials are only needed once a firm, size and structure are agreed in writing. Identity documents, bank details and broker passwords are never requested, at any stage.
