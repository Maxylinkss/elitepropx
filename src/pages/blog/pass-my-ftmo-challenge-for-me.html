What the arrangement actually is

Having someone pass an FTMO challenge for you means a third party trades the evaluation on your account and returns a funded account in your name. The arrangement is only legitimate if FTMO's terms permit third-party trading and the permission covers the work being done. FTMO's terms are strict about account access and control, so the first step is not choosing a service but confirming the clause.

A service that claims to pass FTMO challenges should be able to quote the relevant term or explain why the arrangement is permitted. If it cannot, the risk is not just a lost fee: a payout can be refused and the account can be closed after the pass. The fee is small compared with a funded account that cannot be withdrawn from.

The process a legitimate service follows

The service reads the current FTMO rulebook, builds a risk plan against the static floor and the equity daily stop, and trades the evaluation to the target. It should report progress and should not take a share of the funded profit. The account should remain in the client's name, and the client should retain ownership of the login and the payout details.

A flat fee with retries included is easier to audit than a profit split. Ask what happens after a breach: is the retry covered, discounted or charged in full? Ask for the refund policy in writing. A service that is confident in its process should be willing to put the policy on paper.

When to trade it yourself

If you have a tested method and the time to trade the evaluation, self-trading is usually cheaper. The service is a fit for a trader who values time, has a firm that permits the arrangement and does not want to manage the evaluation personally. It is not a substitute for a strategy: the funded account still has to be traded after the pass, and the service does not do that.

Before paying, confirm the fee, the retry policy, the terms check and the timeline. A service that cannot answer all four is not ready to be hired.

Common questions

Is it allowed to have someone pass my FTMO challenge?

Only if FTMO terms permit third-party trading and the permission covers the work. Confirm the clause in writing before paying a service; a prohibited arrangement can lead to a closed account and a refused payout.

Does the service take a share of the funded profit?

A legitimate flat-fee service should not. The funded account remains in your name and the profit belongs to you. A profit split is a different product with different incentives.

What happens if the service fails the challenge?

It depends on the retry and refund policy. Ask for it in writing before paying. A bundled retry is only valuable if the terms say the second attempt is covered.
