How to Keep Funded Account

What this guide is really solving

Keeping a funded account is a compliance exercise as much as a trading one. The loss floor remains, the conduct rules are usually stricter, and the first payout is the point at which the process is proven. On an evaluation, the consequence is specific: the same decision either keeps the loss floor at a distance or brings the end of the account closer. The sections below turn that into a plan rather than a principle.

The rules that still apply after the pass

A funded account is not an unregulated personal account. The loss floor, the daily stop, the consistency condition and the conduct rules still apply, and some of them change after the pass. The most common way to lose a funded account is to trade it as if the evaluation were over. The evaluation was the application; the funded account is the job.

Re-read the funded agreement on the day the account is issued. Confirm the floor definition, the daily reset time, the news policy and the payout conditions. Save a copy with the date. A rule change during the funded period is easier to handle when the original terms are documented.

Withdrawal policy as a risk control

Decide a withdrawal rhythm before the first funded trade. A trader who withdraws every payout and leaves a fixed buffer is less likely to give back a large gain than one who lets the balance grow and increases size with it. The buffer should be large enough to absorb a normal losing streak without approaching the floor.

Do not withdraw to the minimum allowed balance just because the plan permits it. The minimum is the firm's limit, not the trader's risk limit. A buffer of two to three times the normal monthly drawdown is a more sensible target. The account should be able to survive a bad month without a reset.

The conduct rules that end accounts

Account sharing, copy trading, prohibited automation and news-window violations can end a funded account even when the trading is profitable. The funded terms often apply these rules more strictly than the evaluation. If a strategy depends on a grey area, get a written answer before the first funded trade. A profitable account that cannot be withdrawn is not a funded account.

Keep the login private, keep the records clean and keep the terms saved. The operational discipline is as important as the trading edge after the pass.

Common questions

Can I lose a funded account after passing?

Yes. The loss floor, daily stop, consistency and conduct rules still apply, and some are stricter after the pass. Trading the funded account like a personal account is the most common way to lose it.

How much should I leave in the account?

A buffer large enough to absorb a normal losing streak, usually two to three times the expected monthly drawdown, above the plan’s minimum balance.

Do evaluation rules apply to the funded account?

Not always. The funded agreement can change the floor, the daily stop, the news policy and the payout conditions. Read it on the day the account is issued.
