Two futures rulebooks with different daily controls

Topstep and Tradify both evaluate futures accounts on a monthly subscription and a trailing threshold, so the headline rows look alike. The difference is the daily control. Topstep applies a per-session dollar stop and a minimum session count, and its payout stage considers the best day. Tradify combines a trailing floor with a session stop and a simpler qualifying-day path. For a trader who has one bad session, the daily stop is the rule that decides whether the account survives the week.

Topstep's best-day expectation is the row most traders miss. A payout can be reduced if one day produced an outsized share of the profit, even when the account passed cleanly. Tradify's payout conditions are different, so a trader with a lumpy distribution should compare the payout rules before the platform list.

A $50,000 Combine with a $2,000 red day

On a $50,000 account with a $2,000 daily stop, a single session that reaches the stop ends trading for the day. The trailing floor may still be $2,500 away, but the daily rule binds first. That is a feature for a trader who struggles to stop and a constraint for a trader whose method needs a wide intraday range.

The trailing floor then interacts with the next session. If the account made $1,000 the day before, the floor has moved up, so the remaining room is smaller than it was at the start. A trader who keeps the same size after a good day is taking more risk relative to the floor, not less. Recalculate the size from the remaining room before the next session.

Both firms charge monthly, so a slow pass pays for calendar time. A trader who expects to take two months should add two subscription payments to the cost, not just the first one.

Which platform and rulebook fit

Topstep fits a futures trader who wants a proprietary platform, a clear educational path and a hard daily stop. Tradify fits a trader who wants TradingView execution and a simpler qualifying-day structure. A trader who relies on TradingView should check that the order routing is supported end to end, not just that the chart loads.

If both rulebooks fit, choose on the payout condition that would affect your distribution. A trader with one or two large days should prefer the firm whose payout rule does not penalize that pattern. A trader with a steady daily result can treat the payout rule as neutral and choose on platform and price.

Common questions

Does Topstep have a daily loss limit?

Yes. Topstep applies a per-session dollar stop in addition to the trailing threshold. The daily stop can end a session before the trailing floor is reached.

What is a best-day expectation?

It is a payout condition that limits how much of the profit can come from a single day. A profitable account with one outsized day can still see a reduced payout if the rule applies.

Which firm supports TradingView?

Tradify lists TradingView among its platforms. Topstep uses TopstepX and also supports NinjaTrader, Tradovate and Rithmic, but the platform list should be confirmed for the exact plan.
