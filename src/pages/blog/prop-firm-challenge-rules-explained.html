Evaluation rules are not funded rules

The rules that apply during an evaluation are not always the rules that apply after the pass. A firm can allow a strategy on the evaluation and restrict it on the funded account, or apply a consistency condition only at payout. The challenge rules explain the path to the funded account; the funded terms explain what the account is worth. A trader who reads only the challenge page has read half the contract.

The difference shows up in three places: the consistency condition, the news policy and the payout schedule. Each can change after the pass. Before buying, read both pages and write down the clauses that differ. If the difference is material to the strategy, the product is not a fit even if the challenge itself is easy.

The four numbers in every challenge

Every challenge can be reduced to four numbers: the profit target, the maximum loss, the daily loss and the minimum days. The first two define the distance, the third defines the worst day, and the fourth defines the calendar. A rulebook that does not state all four clearly is a rulebook to avoid until support answers in writing.

Convert each number into dollars on the account size you are considering. A 10% target on a $50,000 account is $5,000; a 5% daily stop is $2,500. Then divide by the risk unit to see how many losses the day can absorb. If the answer is fewer than three, the size is too large for the rulebook, regardless of how good the setup looks.

How to verify a rule before trading

Find the clause in the terms, copy it into your notes, and ask support to confirm your interpretation in one sentence. For example: "On the $100,000 plan, is the daily loss measured on equity and does it reset at 00:00 server time?" A specific question gets a specific answer. A general question gets a marketing reply.

Do this for the loss limits, the consistency rule and the payout schedule. Those three cover most of the money at risk. The rest of the rulebook can be read once and checked before the session.

Common questions

Are funded account rules the same as challenge rules?

Not always. Consistency, news and payout conditions can change after the pass. Read both the challenge page and the funded terms before buying.

What are the four key challenge numbers?

The profit target, the maximum loss, the daily loss and the minimum trading days. Convert each into dollars and dates on the account size you are considering.

How do I confirm an ambiguous rule?

Ask support a specific question that names the measurement, the reset time and the consequence. Keep the written answer with your account records.
