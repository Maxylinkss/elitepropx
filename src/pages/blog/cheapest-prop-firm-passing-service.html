Cheap is a total, not a sticker

The cheapest passing service is not the one with the lowest quoted fee. It is the one with the lowest total cost of reaching a funded account that can actually pay out. That total includes the service fee, any evaluation fee not covered, the retry policy and the time to the pass. A $150 service that requires a new evaluation fee for every attempt can cost more than a $220 service with retries included.

The terms check is part of the cost. A service that does not verify whether the firm permits third-party trading is cheap only until the payout is refused. At that point the fee is lost and the account may be closed. The cheapest route is the one that survives the payout review.

The three questions that change the price

First, does the fee include the evaluation or does the client buy it separately? Second, what happens after a breach: is the retry included, discounted or charged in full? Third, is the fee flat or does the service take a share of the funded profit? A flat fee with included retries is the easiest structure to compare. A profit split is a different product with a different long-term cost.

Ask for the answers in writing. A quote that says "cheapest" without specifying the inclusions is not a quote; it is an advertisement. The total cost can be calculated only when the inclusions are known.

When paying more is cheaper

A higher fee with included retries can be cheaper than a lower fee with per-attempt charges if the expected number of attempts is greater than one. A service that includes the evaluation can be cheaper than one that does not, even if the headline fee is higher. A service that checks the terms can be cheaper than one that ignores them, because a refused payout costs the entire account.

Start with the smallest viable account and the service with the clearest policy. The first engagement is a test of the process, not a search for the lowest price on the page.

Common questions

What is the cheapest passing service?

The one with the lowest total cost across the expected attempts, not the lowest quoted fee. Include the evaluation fee, retry policy and terms compliance in the comparison.

Are retries included?

It depends on the service. A bundled retry is cheaper only if the terms say the second attempt is covered without another fee. Ask before paying.

Can a cheap service be risky?

Yes. A service that does not check whether the firm permits third-party trading is cheap only until the payout is refused. Terms compliance is part of the cost.
