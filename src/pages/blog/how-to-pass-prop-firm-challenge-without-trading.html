The legitimate meaning of without trading

Passing a challenge without trading usually means hiring a service to trade the evaluation on your behalf, not finding a way to skip the rules. The distinction matters because the rules still apply to the account. A service cannot make a prohibited arrangement permitted, and a pass obtained through a breach can be reversed at payout.

The legitimate route is a third-party trading service where the firm's terms allow it. The service trades the evaluation, returns the account in the client's name and does not take a share of the funded profit. If the terms do not allow third-party trading, the correct answer is to trade it yourself or choose a firm that permits the arrangement.

What to check before hiring

Check four things: the firm's third-party trading clause, the service's fee and retry policy, the timeline and the ownership of the funded account. The clause is the most important because it decides whether the pass is durable. A service that does not check the clause is selling a pass that may not survive a payout review.

The fee should be flat and the retry policy written down. A profit split or a percentage of the funded account is a different product; it changes the incentive and usually means the service has a claim on the account after the pass. The account should remain in the client's name, with the client controlling the payout details.

The alternatives

If the firm prohibits third-party trading, the alternatives are to trade the evaluation yourself with a smaller risk unit, to choose a firm that permits the arrangement, or to use a demo account to build the record before paying for another evaluation. The last option is slower but preserves the fee. A trader who has failed the same rulebook three times should treat the next attempt as a process problem, not a motivation problem.

There is no legitimate way to skip the risk rules. A service that promises otherwise is selling a breach, not a pass.

Common questions

Can I pass a prop firm challenge without trading?

You can hire a third-party service to trade it for you, but only if the firm permits that arrangement. There is no legitimate way to bypass the loss rules or the evaluation itself.

Is a passing service allowed by all firms?

No. Some firms prohibit third-party trading and treat it as account sharing. Check the terms before paying a service.

What should the service cost?

A flat fee with the retry policy written down is the easiest structure to audit. A profit split is a different product and should be evaluated as such.
