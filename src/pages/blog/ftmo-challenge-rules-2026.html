The three numbers that define an FTMO attempt

FTMO's rulebook is built on three numbers: the maximum loss, the daily loss and the profit target. The maximum loss is static and measured from the starting balance, so profit does not move the failure point. The daily loss is measured on equity, which means open positions count against it. The target is the distance the account has to travel before the next phase. A trader who knows only the target has not read the rulebook.

The verification phase adds a fourth condition: the account has to repeat the performance after the evaluation phases. That resets the calendar even after a clean pass. Budget for the extra phase before buying, because a strategy that needs a fast payout is a different product from one that can wait.

Sizing with an equity daily stop

On a $100,000 account with a 5% daily stop, the session limit is $5,000. At a 1% risk unit of $1,000, five losses reach the limit. At 0.5%, ten losses do. The equity measurement means an open position that moves against you reduces the remaining daily allowance even before it is closed. A trader who opens three positions at once is using the same daily budget three times, not diversifying it.

The total floor is $10,000 on a 10% plan. Twenty losses at $500 reach it, but the daily stop will usually end the session first. That is why the daily rule, not the total rule, should drive the session plan. The total floor is the constraint on the month; the daily stop is the constraint on the day.

Conduct rules and the payout stage

FTMO prohibits practices such as account sharing, copy trading between clients, and strategies that exploit latency or platform errors. The evaluation itself has no consistency rule, but the payout stage has its own conditions. Read the funded-account terms as carefully as the evaluation terms, because a rule that does not apply during the challenge can still affect the withdrawal.

If a strategy depends on holding through news, confirm the current news policy. If it depends on a specific platform feature, confirm that the feature is available on the account type. The terms change between plan generations, so the page you read last year is not evidence of the rule today.

Common questions

Is the FTMO maximum loss static or trailing?

It is static and measured from the starting balance. Profit does not move the failure point, which makes a fixed risk unit valid for the whole attempt.

Is the daily loss measured on balance or equity?

It is measured on equity, so open positions count against the daily allowance. A floating loss reduces the room available for the session.

Does FTMO have a consistency rule?

The evaluation does not apply a consistency rule. The funded account and payout terms should still be read separately, because conditions can differ after the pass.
