The return on the fee, honestly

The 2026 cost stack, not the headline fee

A passing service quote is only one line of the cost stack. The full cost of reaching a first payout includes the service fee, the evaluation fee if the service does not cover it, any reset or retry charge, platform and data costs, and the time between payment and the funded account. A $220 service fee attached to a $500 evaluation and a $60 data feed is not a $220 route; it is a $780 route before the first withdrawal.

The 2026 difference is that more firms now charge monthly subscriptions and more services bundle retries. A bundled retry is valuable only if the service actually covers a second attempt without another fee. Ask what happens after a breach: is the retry included, discounted or charged in full? That answer changes the expected cost more than a small difference in the headline price.

Cost per funded account across three routes

Route one is self-trading with one attempt. The cost is the evaluation fee plus platform costs for the attempt. Route two is self-trading with an expected three attempts. The cost is three fees plus three months of platform costs, minus any reset discount. Route three is a flat service with retries included. The cost is the service fee plus any evaluation fee not covered, and it does not grow with the number of attempts the service needs.

If the service fee is $220 and the evaluation is $100, route three costs $320 for a funded account. If the self-trading route takes three attempts at $100 plus $60 of platform costs, it costs $480 and three months. The service is cheaper in that example, but only because the expected attempt count is three. A trader who passes first time spends $160 and is better off self-trading. The honest comparison uses your own pass probability, not the industry average.

What a 2026 quote should disclose

A quote should state the fee, what it covers, the retry policy, the expected timeline and the firms it applies to. It should also state the terms check: whether the firm permits third-party trading and whether the permission covers the funded account. A quote that omits the terms check is not a complete quote, because a refused payout can turn a cheap route into an expensive one.

Ask for the refund policy in writing. If the service cannot pass the evaluation, does it refund, retry or credit the fee? The answer should be in the terms, not in a chat message. A service that is confident in its process should be willing to put the policy on paper.

Common questions

What does a passing service cost in 2026?

Flat fees are commonly quoted around a few hundred dollars, with $220 as the reference point on this site. The total route also includes any evaluation fee, data costs and the time to a funded account.

Are retries included in the fee?

It depends on the service. A bundled retry is valuable only if the terms say the second attempt is covered without another fee. Ask for the policy in writing.

Is a passing service cheaper than self-trading?

Only if your expected number of attempts is high enough. A trader who passes first time is usually cheaper self-trading; a trader who expects three attempts may find the flat fee cheaper.
