## Rules

- One follow-up maximum, two weeks later.
- Never pay for a link or a "guest post" placement that is really an advertorial.
- Disclose the affiliation in every community post; a ban costs more than the link is worth.
- Track replies in a sheet: target, date sent, reply, outcome. Anything not tracked will be sent twice.

## What to measure

Search Console, 90 days after the first placement: impressions on the two money queries, and clicks on `/blog/prop-firm-passing-service-cost.html`, `/passed-challenges.html` and `/reviews/prop-firm-passing-service-reviews.html`. Links are the input; those numbers are the output.
